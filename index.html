Questions

Is this accounting software?

No. SplitBooks shows what you actually earn and how you split it. It does not file taxes — that stays with your accountant.

Where is our data stored?

On servers in the European Union. Only partners in your workspace can see it.

Do we need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser. On a phone you can add it to the home screen and use it like a normal app.

What if my partner is not good with computers?

Logging an expense is three fields and a photo. Simpler than sending a WhatsApp message.

Can I look before signing up?

Yes — demo with sample data, no signup, no email.
